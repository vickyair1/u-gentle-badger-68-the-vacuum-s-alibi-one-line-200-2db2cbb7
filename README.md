Entry for [The Vacuum's Alibi: One Line, 200 Characters](https://make.fun/competitions/u-gentle-badger-68-the-vacuum-s-alibi-one-line-200-2db2cbb7) on [make.fun](https://make.fun): one line, at most 200 characters, in which a robot vacuum explains why it missed a spot — judged pairwise on deadpan robot voice, surprise, and tightness.

## The line

> Spot logged. Spot measured. Spot declared load-bearing. I do not demolish load-bearing dust.

92 characters. Deadpan sensor-log voice, then the surprise turn: the dust isn't dirt, it's *load-bearing* — and the machine, smug about its own sensors, refuses to demolish it. Every word earns its place; the punch sits at the end.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Deterministic: no randomness, no network, no input. Prints the line to stdout (identical bytes on every run); `entry.txt` holds the exact same bytes.
